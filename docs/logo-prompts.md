# Prompt Logo RetPAP

Logo harus satu ide, bentuk sederhana, tetap terbaca di 32px. Semua prompt
berbahasa Inggris karena model gambar lebih patuh.

## Prompt utama — aperture + spark (ikon aplikasi)

> Minimalist flat vector app icon for a photo-rating app. One single idea: a
> geometric camera aperture made of six rounded blades, its center hole shaped
> like a small four-point spark. Deep navy circular background (#0b0f19), the
> blades in flat cyan (#22d3ee) with one blade in amber (#fbbf24) as the
> accent. Thick even line weights, hard edges, two flat colors only, no
> gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no text. Centered composition with generous
> padding, symmetrical, crisp and readable at 32px. Square 1:1, plain
> background, vector style.

## Alternatif 2 — monogram P (paling mudah diingat)

> Minimalist logo mark, letter "P" built from a rounded rectangle frame and a
> camera aperture cut into its counter. Flat vector, single weight stroke, deep
> navy background, cyan mark with one amber dot at the aperture center.
> Geometric, balanced, no text, no gradients, no shadows, works as a 512x512
> app icon and as a tiny 24px favicon.

## Alternatif 3 — es → api → petir (nyambung ke tier rating)

> Simple flat icon: a hexagonal shield outline containing three stacked shapes
> — a snowflake tip, a flame body, a lightning tail — merged into one
> continuous silhouette. Flat vector, two colors max: cyan and amber on deep
> navy. Bold simple geometry, no fine detail, no text, no gradients, centered,
> readable at small sizes.

## Negative prompt (tempel di semua)

> text, letters, words, watermark, photorealistic, 3D render, bevel, drop
> shadow, glow, gradient mesh, detailed texture, thin hairlines, clutter,
> multiple icons, mockup, phone frame, perspective, noisy background

## Catatan teknis

- Ukuran `1024x1024`, latar polos (transparan atau `#0b0f19`), objek di tengah
  dengan padding sekitar 15%.
- Maksimal 2–3 warna supaya tetap terbaca di avatar bot 64px dan favicon 32px.
- Kalau hasilnya masih ramai, tambahkan: `simplify to a single silhouette,
  maximum 5 shapes`.
- Simpan versi tanpa teks; nama "RetPAP" cukup di UI, jangan di dalam gambar.
